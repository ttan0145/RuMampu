Language: **English** | [Chinese (CN)](US2.3_LOWER_INCOME.cn.md)

| Acceptance criterion | Status | Implementation and evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AC2.3.1 Use the RuMampu low income rule | Passed | With at least two completed months, the service marks every month tied at the recorded usable-income minimum; one month produces no comparison conclusion. |
| AC2.3.2 Explain how lower income months are identified | Passed | The screen says the result is the lowest usable-income month in the completed record and is neither a financial standard nor a prediction. |
| AC2.3.3 Leave the unfinished month out of the count [v24] NEW | Passed | The pattern and housing pre-check exclude the running month; pattern and coverage responses return it separately as month so far. |

Tests cover a unique minimum, tied minima, one month, negative usable income, and a current month with dated work costs. The 12-month scenario identifies only `2026-02`. No fixed percentage, coefficient score, or risk label is returned.
